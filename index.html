Under the Hood
1
PMI Weighting
Eliminates boilerplate skills that appear everywhere (e.g. ‘communication skills’). Only statistically significant co-occurrences surface.
2
Corpus-Size Gate
A skill must appear in 50 or more JDs for your role-seniority tier before it becomes a signal. No single-listing noise.
3
Prevalence Floor
A high PMI score on a skill mentioned in only one or two JDs is noise, not signal — so anything under 10% prevalence is held back as ‘Insufficient Data’ regardless of its PMI score.
4
Seniority Segmentation
Fresher SDE signals are separate from Senior SDE signals. You see the right tier, not a mixed corpus.
